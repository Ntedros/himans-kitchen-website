# Himan's Kitchen website

Website for **Himan's Kitchen**, a family-run Eritrean and Egyptian restaurant at 480 Portage Ave in Winnipeg.

**Live site:** [himanskitchen.com](https://himanskitchen.com)

## What it does

- Shows the menu, with house specialties like tibs, ful and the tsom platter
- Explains catering and Habesha party trays, with the order and deposit policy
- Takes pickup orders online through Square
- Collects questions and catering requests through contact forms
- Shows food and dining room photos in a gallery

## How it's built

- **Platform:** WordPress.com, with content exported here for import
- **Ordering:** Square online ordering, set to pickup only and limited to opening hours
- **Delivery:** DoorDash and Uber Eats listings
- **Search:** Google Business Profile and local directory listings, plus image alt text and page SEO
